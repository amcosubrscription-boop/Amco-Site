About Ruchi Agarwal

CA Ruchi Agarwal Partner CA Ruchi Agarwal is the Partner at Agarwal Maheswari & Co. He is a Qualified Chartered Accountant and Company Secretary and has completed her graduation in Commerce with First Class Honours from Kolkata University. Ruchi has successfully completed her three years of articleship performing audits of different sectors. With more than five years of experience in the fields of audits, her other areas of expertise include INDAS and NBFC compliances. She is actively involved in audit and assurance services viz. statutory audit, internal audit and tax audit. She had also recently authored a published fiction novel.
